# Experiment — Bread Machine White Bread — Baseline

## Metadata

- Related Recipe: [Bread Machine White Bread](../../../recipes/american/breads/bread-machine/bread-machine-white-bread.md)
- Date: 26 Sep 2026
- Status: In Progress
- Machine: Zojirushi BB-PDC20
- Version: Baseline / first test

## Hypothesis

A simple water-based white-bread formula can establish a reliable baseline loaf on Course 1 of the Zojirushi BB-PDC20 before testing milk or other enrichment changes.

## Variables

- Changed: None; this establishes the baseline.
- Held constant: Zojirushi BB-PDC20, Course 1 — Basic White, Medium crust, ingredient-loading order.

## Required Equipment

- Zojirushi BB-PDC20 bread machine

## Inputs

| Input | Value |
|---|---|
| Water | 1 cup + 2 tbsp, lukewarm |
| Unsalted butter | 2 tbsp, softened |
| Sugar | 2 tbsp |
| Salt | 1 1/2 tsp |
| Bread flour | 3 cups |
| Instant or bread-machine yeast | 2 tsp |
| Course | 1 — Basic White |
| Crust | Medium |
| Delay timer | Off |

### Loading Order

1. Water
2. Butter
3. Sugar
4. Salt
5. Bread flour
6. Yeast in a shallow well on top of the flour

## Observations

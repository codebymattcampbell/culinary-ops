# Peri-Peri Spatchcock Chicken v1

## Objective
Create a juicy, crisp-skinned spatchcock chicken on the small ceramic Egg using a dry-brined skin, a thin peri-peri-mayo coating, and a hot indirect cook.

## Status
In progress

## Equipment
- Small ceramic Egg / kamado
- Heat deflector for indirect cooking
- Instant-read thermometer
- Wire rack and sheet pan
- Blender or food processor
- Kitchen shears

## Variables
- Chicken: whole chicken, spatchcocked
- Dry brine: 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt + 1 tsp aluminum-free baking powder
- Dry-brine time: about 45-60 minutes uncovered in refrigerator
- Sauce chile base: 2 chipotle chiles in adobo + 1 tbsp adobo sauce
- Sauce: garlic, lemon juice, red wine vinegar, smoked paprika, oregano, cumin, tomato paste, olive oil, black pepper
- Skin coating: 2 tbsp peri-peri sauce + 1 tbsp mayonnaise, spread very thinly
- Underside: light coating of peri-peri sauce
- Smoke wood: pecan chips, with optional small hickory chunk
- Cooker setup: indirect
- Dome target: 375-400°F, aiming near 400°F
- First temperature check: about 30 minutes after chicken goes on
- Pull target, breast: 155-157°F in the coolest part
- Thigh target: 170°F+
- Rest: 10-15 minutes uncovered

## Timeline
- Chicken prepped and dry brined before cooker heat-up
- Egg heat-up was slower than expected; approximately 200°F to 310°F took around 40-45 minutes
- Wood and heat deflector added around 300°F
- Chicken held refrigerated until cooker approached target temperature
- Final cook time: TBD

## Hypothesis
A baking-powder dry brine will improve skin drying and browning, while a very thin mayonnaise-peri-peri layer will improve adhesion and browning without trapping enough moisture to soften the skin. Cooking indirect near 400°F should render and crisp the skin while allowing the breast to be pulled in the mid-150s before drying out.

## Observations
- Pecan available as small chips rather than chunks.
- Charcoal bed used large lump pieces and appeared to have good visible airflow.
- Cooker temperature climbed more slowly than expected.
- Peri-peri coating was applied as a thin film with skin texture still clearly visible.

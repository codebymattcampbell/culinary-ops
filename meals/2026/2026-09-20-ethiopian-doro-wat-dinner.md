# Ethiopian Doro Wat Dinner

A complete Ethiopian-inspired dinner built around Doro Wat, fermented teff injera, and gomen.

## Date

20 Sep 2026

## Status

**Tested**

## Full Meal

- [Doro Wat](../../recipes/chicken/doro-wat.md)
- [Teff Injera](../../recipes/breads/teff-injera.md)
- [Gomen](../../recipes/vegetables/gomen.md)

## Cook Notes

- Doro Wat was made with boneless skinless chicken thighs, unsalted butter, and no eggs.
- The Instant Pot was used as a sauté/simmer vessel rather than for pressure cooking.
- Injera fermented successfully for about 24 hours and produced abundant eyes after an absit step.
- The first injera was too thick and dried on the surface while remaining gooey inside.
- Adding 1 tbsp water to the remaining batter and using a shorter covered steam produced a much better result.
- Gomen used the remaining bunch of collard greens and provided a mild counterpoint to the Doro Wat.
- Overall meal received great reviews.

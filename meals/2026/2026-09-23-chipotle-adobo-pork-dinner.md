## Cook Notes

- About 3 lb of a 5.49 lb package of boneless pork shoulder country-style ribs was used; the remaining pork was reserved for a separate black-lime meal.
- Pork went onto the Egg at 2:09 PM and reached 155°F at about 3:04 PM.
- The pork was smoked at 250-275°F, then transferred to a covered Lodge 8DO Dutch oven for the braise.
- The Lodge 8DO does not fit in the small Egg, so the braise was completed in the kitchen oven at 325°F.
- Pork was finished by reducing the braising liquid on the stovetop, breaking the meat into large chunks, and returning it to the sauce.
- Dried pigeon peas were pressure-cooked separately before being cooked a second time with the rice.
- The carrots shared the 325°F oven with the pork. They needed more time than originally planned; future shared-oven cooks should allow about 50-60 minutes or finish them at 425°F.
- Overall meal received great reviews.

# Pineapple-Mango Salsa

A bright pineapple, mango, lime, cilantro, and red-onion salsa designed to cut through rich jerk-seasoned meats and coconut rice.

## Status

**Tested**

## Yield

- Servings: 3-4 as a condiment

## Required Equipment

- Cutting board and knife
- Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups fresh pineapple, cut into 1/4-inch dice
- 1 ripe mango, cut into 1/4-inch dice
- 1/4 red onion, very finely diced
- 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- 1/8 tsp Scotch bonnet mash
- Lime zest, optional

## Method

1. Combine the pineapple, mango, red onion, cilantro, lime juice, salt, and Scotch bonnet mash.
2. Mix thoroughly and taste.
3. Adjust lime, salt, or heat if needed.
4. Refrigerate for 30-60 minutes before serving when time allows.

## Quality Targets

- Bright and acidic
- Distinct pieces of pineapple and mango rather than a wet puree
- Mild-to-moderate chile warmth
- Enough acidity to balance rich meat and coconut rice

## Notes

- Start conservatively with Scotch bonnet mash. It is easy to increase the heat after tasting.
- Best made shortly before the meal rather than far in advance.

## Variables to Record

- Fruit ripeness
- Lime amount
- Scotch bonnet amount
- Salt level
- Rest time

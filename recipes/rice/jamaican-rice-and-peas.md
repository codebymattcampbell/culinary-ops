# Jamaican Rice and Peas

Coconut rice with pigeon peas, scallion, thyme, garlic, and allspice. This Instant Pot version uses dried pigeon peas cooked separately before the rice so both components reach the right texture.

## Status

**Tested**

## Yield

- Servings: 5-6 as a side

## Required Equipment

- [Instant Pot](../../equipment/instant-pot.md)
- Fine-mesh strainer
- Measuring cup

## Ingredients

### Pigeon Peas
- 1 1/2 cups dried pigeon peas
- 4 1/2 cups water

### Rice
- 2 cups extra-long-grain white rice, rinsed well
- Cooked pigeon peas from above
- 1 can (13.5 oz) coconut milk
- About 1/3 cup reserved pigeon-pea cooking liquid, enough to bring coconut milk plus pea liquid to 2 cups total
- 2 scallions, sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 tsp thyme leaves
- 1/4 tsp ground allspice
- 1 tsp kosher salt

## Method

1. Add the dried pigeon peas and 4 1/2 cups water to the Instant Pot.
2. Cook on High Pressure for 15 minutes, followed by a 15-minute natural release. Vent any remaining pressure.
3. Turn off Keep Warm if the peas will sit before the rice is started. They may remain in their cooking liquid temporarily.
4. Drain the peas, reserving the cooking liquid.
5. Add the rinsed rice and cooked pigeon peas to the Instant Pot.
6. Add the 13.5 oz can of coconut milk. Add enough reserved pea liquid to make 2 cups total combined liquid; this is approximately 1/3 cup pea liquid.
7. Add the scallions, garlic, thyme, allspice, and salt.
8. Cook on High Pressure for 4 minutes.
9. Allow a 10-minute natural release, then vent the remaining pressure.
10. Fluff gently and rest uncovered for about 5 minutes before serving.

## Quality Targets

- Rice grains tender and separate rather than wet or gummy
- Pigeon peas fully tender but still intact
- Coconut present but balanced by savory aromatics
- Thyme and allspice noticeable without dominating

## Notes

- The tested ratio of 1 1/2 cups dried pigeon peas to 2 cups rice is intentionally pea-forward.
- Do not add 2 cups pea liquid in addition to the coconut milk. Coconut milk plus reserved pea liquid should equal 2 cups total liquid.
- The leftover pea cooking liquid can be used a few tablespoons at a time to loosen greens or another savory side.
- Scotch bonnet can be added in a very small amount, but it was not necessary to make this meal work.

## Variables to Record

- Pigeon-pea tenderness after first pressure cook
- Rice brand
- Exact measured coconut-milk volume
- Exact pea-liquid addition
- Final rice texture
- Preferred rice-to-pea ratio

# Mexican-Style Pigeon Pea Rice

Savory Instant Pot rice with dried pigeon peas, onion, garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, and oregano. The pigeon peas are pressure-cooked first, then finish with the rice so they become tender without turning to mush.

## Status

**Tested**

## Yield

- Servings: 4-5 as a side

## Required Equipment

- [Instant Pot](../../equipment/instant-pot.md)
- Fine-mesh strainer
- Measuring cups

## Advance Prep

- No soaking is required with the tested Instant Pot method.
- The dried pigeon peas must be pressure-cooked before the rice. Start them first so the rice does not become the timing bottleneck.
- Plan on roughly 45-60 minutes for the peas including pressure buildup and natural release, followed by the rice cook.

## Ingredients

### Pigeon Peas

- 3/4 cup dried pigeon peas
- 3 cups water

### Rice

- 1 1/2 cups long-grain white rice, rinsed until the water runs mostly clear and drained well
- Partially cooked pigeon peas from above, drained
- 1 1/2 cups chicken stock
- 1/2 medium yellow onion, finely diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp butter or oil
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, reduced if the stock is salty
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

## Method

### Partially Cook the Pigeon Peas

1. Rinse the dried pigeon peas.
2. Add the peas and 3 cups water to the Instant Pot.
3. Cook on High Pressure for 20-22 minutes.
4. Allow a 10-15 minute natural release, then vent any remaining pressure.
5. Drain the peas. They should be mostly tender but retain enough structure to survive the second pressure cook.

### Cook the Rice

1. Set the Instant Pot to Sauté.
2. Add the butter or oil and diced onion. Cook about 3 minutes until the onion begins to soften.
3. Add the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds.
4. Add the cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, and black pepper. Stir for 20-30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Add the rinsed rice, chicken stock, drained pigeon peas, and salt.
6. Stir once to distribute the ingredients and scrape the bottom so nothing is stuck.
7. Cook on High Pressure for 4 minutes.
8. Allow a 10-minute natural release, then vent the remaining pressure.
9. Fluff gently and rest about 5 minutes before serving.

## Quality Targets

- Rice grains tender and separate rather than wet or gummy
- Pigeon peas fully tender but still intact
- Savory cumin and paprika flavor that supports rather than overwhelms a strongly seasoned main dish
- Enough salt to season the rice while leaving room for a saucy braised meat

## Notes

- Tested 23 Sep 2026 with chipotle-adobo smoked-braised pork and received great reviews.
- The recipe deliberately uses dried pigeon peas rather than canned.
- Cooking the peas separately first gives much better control over both pea and rice texture.
- Salsa and the pork braising sauce provide additional acidity and moisture at service.

# Beef and Mushroom Cottage Pie

A practical oven dinner for ground beef, mushrooms, small potatoes, and frozen broccoli. The potato topping will be thin in a 9 × 13-inch dish; use a smaller deep dish only if the filling fits below the rim.

## Status

**Experimental** — planned recipe, not yet cooked and adjusted.

## Yield

- 4–6 servings
- About 65–75 minutes, including a 10-minute rest

## Required Equipment

- [Oven](../../equipment/oven.md)
- Large skillet, pot, potato masher, and 9 × 13-inch baking dish

## Ingredients

- 2 lb lean ground beef, thawed
- About 8 small yellow or red potatoes (roughly 1½–2 lb), cut into 1-inch chunks
- 1 onion, diced
- 8–12 oz mushrooms, sliced, or however many are on hand
- 10 oz frozen broccoli
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 cup beef broth, or 1 cup water plus beef bouillon
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, optional
- 2–3 tbsp butter
- 2–4 tbsp milk, added as needed
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp dried thyme, optional
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- Shredded cheese for the topping, optional

## Method

1. Heat the oven to **400°F**. Put the potatoes in a pot, cover with cold water, salt the water, and boil until easily pierced with a fork, about **12–18 minutes** after the water reaches a boil.
2. While the potatoes cook, brown the beef and onion in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking up the meat. If the skillet is crowded, brown the beef in two batches. Pour off excess fat, leaving about 1 tbsp in the pan.
3. Add the mushrooms. Cook until their released liquid has mostly evaporated. Season with the garlic powder, thyme if using, and black pepper. Start with **1 tsp salt** if using unsalted broth, or less if using salted broth or bouillon.
4. Sprinkle the flour over the filling and stir for **1 minute**. Gradually stir in the broth and Worcestershire if using. Simmer **3–5 minutes**, until the filling is moist and thickened, with no watery pool at the bottom. Taste and adjust salt and pepper.
5. Thaw the broccoli in the microwave or briefly in simmering water; drain it thoroughly. Chop any large florets and stir into the beef filling.
6. Drain the potatoes and let them steam dry for a minute. Mash with butter and just enough milk to make a spreadable but fairly thick topping. Season with salt and pepper.
7. Spread the filling in the baking dish. Spoon small dollops of potato over the surface, then gently spread them together. Drag a fork across the top for ridges. Sprinkle with cheese if using.
8. Bake **uncovered for 20–25 minutes**, until the filling bubbles around the edges. For more color, broil **2–3 minutes** while watching closely. Rest **10 minutes** before serving.

## Quality Targets

- Filling: beef cooked through; mushrooms browned and no watery liquid in the dish.
- Topping: hot throughout with lightly browned ridges.
- Texture: saucy filling that holds together reasonably well after resting.

## Notes

- With only eight small potatoes, the topping may not completely cover the beef. Spoon it on in patches if necessary; the pie still cooks well.
- If there is no broth or bouillon, use water and season more assertively. Worcestershire helps add savory depth but is optional.
- Refrigerate leftovers promptly in shallow containers. Reheat until hot throughout.

## Variables to Record

- Actual weight of potatoes and mushrooms; whether the topping covered the dish.
- Broth saltiness and final seasoning.
- Filling thickness before baking and moisture after resting.

# Japanese Dashi Cabbage & Mushroom Soup

A light, clear vegetable soup for breakfast meal preparation, with dashi, tender cabbage and mushrooms.

## Status

**Experimental.** Version 1 was completed on 2026-10-25. The method below consolidates the recipe plan; the exact executed sequence, final seasoning and tasting result were not confirmed.

## Yield

- Approximately 6–8 servings planned; actual yield unknown.
- Prep: 10–15 minutes. Cooking: approximately 20–25 minutes, including 18–20 minutes simmering. These are estimates.

## Required Equipment

- Large stockpot and stove
- Knife, cutting board and ladle

## Helpful but Optional

## Ingredients

|---|---|---|

| Cremini mushrooms | 4–5 oz, thinly sliced | Recorded in completed batch |
| Carrots | 3 medium, in ⅛-inch rounds or half-moons | Planned quantity; actual not independently confirmed |

| Low-sodium soy sauce | 1–2 teaspoons, optional finish | Planned; actual use unconfirmed |

## Method

Recipe plan for the next cook; do not treat these steps as a verified reconstruction of v1.

1. Prepare vegetables as listed above.
2. Add 6 cups water to the pot. Prepare dashi with the three recorded packets using the product's extraction directions; remove packets before adding vegetables.
3. Add cabbage, carrots, ginger and scallion whites. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered about **10 minutes**. Avoid a rolling boil.
4. Add both mushrooms. Simmer **8–10 minutes** more, until carrots and cabbage are tender without becoming excessively soft.
5. Taste; add soy sauce if desired. Add scallion greens shortly before serving.
6. Add water or prepared dashi if too vegetable-heavy. A whole cabbage may need **7–8 cups total broth**; this is a future adjustment, not the recorded v1 amount.

## Notes

- Gentle simmering was selected for texture and clarity.
- Garlic was deliberately omitted; rice vinegar and sesame oil were omitted from the final plan.
- Cabbage continues softening during storage and reheating.
- [Dashi product reference](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CP5GBT33): Grelim Dashi Stock Japan Dashi Soup, 8 g packets. The supplied notes identify fish-derived ingredients and kombu; check packaging for allergens, preparation directions, sodium and mineral information. Compare concentration with package directions on future cooks.
- Vegetables provide fiber and naturally occurring minerals. This is not an electrolyte replacement treatment or a complete meal by itself. Recalculate nutrition from actual weights and labels before adding numerical estimates.
- Carrots remain in this soup; the carrots removed on 2026-10-25 were the separate roast side.

## Storage and Reheating

- Divide promptly into shallow containers and refrigerate within two hours (one hour above 90°F). Keep at **40°F / 4°C or below**.
- Use refrigerated portions within **3–4 days**; freeze additional portions.
- Reheat to **165°F / 74°C**; bring soup to a rolling boil when reheating.
- These are future handling instructions. Actual refrigerator placement for v1 was not confirmed.
- [USDA leftovers guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety)

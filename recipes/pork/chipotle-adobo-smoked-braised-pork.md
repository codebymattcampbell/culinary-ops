# Chipotle-Adobo Smoked-Braised Pork

Boneless pork shoulder country-style ribs lightly smoked on the Egg, then braised in a chipotle-adobo sauce until probe-tender. The finished pork is broken into large juicy chunks and tossed back through the reduced braising sauce.

## Status

**Tested**

## Yield

- Servings: 3-4
- Tested with about 3 lb boneless pork shoulder country-style ribs

## Required Equipment

- [Egg](../../equipment/egg.md)
- [Oven](../../equipment/oven.md)
- Heavy Dutch oven with lid
- Instant-read thermometer or probe thermometer

## Advance Prep

- No overnight prep required.
- Allow roughly 3 hours total cook time plus Egg startup and stabilization.
- The Lodge 8DO Dutch oven does not fit the small Egg used for testing, so the tested workflow is smoke on the Egg, then braise in the kitchen oven.

## Ingredients

### Pork and Rub

- About 3 lb boneless pork shoulder country-style ribs
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder

### Braise

- 1 medium yellow onion, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp adobo sauce
- 1 cup chicken stock
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar

## Method

1. Set up the Egg for indirect cooking and stabilize at 250-275°F.
2. Pat the pork dry. Mix the salt, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, black pepper, and garlic powder, then season the pork on all sides. No binder is required.
3. Put the pork directly on the Egg grate and smoke until the thickest pieces reach about 150-160°F internal and have developed good color, roughly 60-90 minutes. The tested cook moved to the braise at 155°F.
4. Heat the kitchen oven to 325°F.
5. Add the sliced onion, garlic, chopped chipotles, adobo sauce, chicken stock, and apple cider vinegar to the Dutch oven and stir.
6. Nestle the smoked pork into the braising liquid. The liquid should reach roughly one-quarter to one-third of the way up the meat rather than submerging it.
7. Cover and braise at 325°F for about 1 1/2-2 hours. Begin checking around 90 minutes.
8. The pork is done when the thickest pieces are roughly 195-205°F and, more importantly, a probe or fork slides in with very little resistance.
9. Transfer the pork to a plate and loosely cover.
10. Put the Dutch oven over medium heat on the stovetop and reduce the braising liquid uncovered for 8-12 minutes, stirring occasionally.
11. Taste the sauce. Add a small splash of apple cider vinegar if it needs brightness, or a little stock or water if it has reduced too far.
12. Break or shred the pork into large juicy chunks rather than fine strands.
13. Return the pork to the reduced sauce, toss to coat, and rest 5-10 minutes before serving.

## Quality Targets

- Distinct smoke flavor without overpowering the chipotle
- Pork tender enough for a probe to slide in easily while still holding juicy chunks
- Sauce reduced enough to cling to the pork but still loose enough to spoon over rice
- Smoky, savory, moderately spicy flavor with enough acidity to keep the pork from tasting heavy

## Notes

- Tested 23 Sep 2026 and received great reviews.
- The smoke-to-braise method worked well: the Egg supplied smoke and exterior flavor, while the covered oven braise provided controlled tenderization.
- Tenderness matters more than the exact final internal temperature.
- Salsa works well as the fresh acidic serving element.
- This recipe was served with Mexican-style pigeon pea rice and cumin-paprika roasted carrots.

# Cumin-Paprika Roasted Carrots

Simple roasted carrots seasoned with cumin, smoked paprika, garlic, salt, and black pepper. Designed as an easy vegetable side for smoky or chile-forward mains.

## Status

**Tested**

## Yield

- Servings: 3-4

## Required Equipment

- [Oven](../../equipment/oven.md)
- Sheet pan
- Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

- 1 bunch carrots
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- Kosher salt, to taste
- Black pepper, to taste

## Method

### Preferred 425°F Method

1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Peel the carrots if desired and cut into roughly 1/2-inch-thick pieces. Split smaller carrots lengthwise as needed so the pieces cook evenly.
3. Toss with the oil, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
4. Spread in a single layer on a sheet pan.
5. Roast for 15 minutes, then stir or flip.
6. Continue roasting another 10-20 minutes, until browned at the edges and a fork slides in with only slight resistance.

### 325°F Shared-Oven Method

1. Prepare and season the carrots as above.
2. Roast at 325°F for about 50-60 minutes, stirring once halfway through.
3. If another dish comes out first, increase the oven to 425°F for the final 5-10 minutes to improve browning.

## Quality Targets

- Fully tender centers with slight resistance rather than crunch
- Browned or lightly blistered edges
- Warm cumin and paprika flavor without a heavy spice coating
- Carrot sweetness still noticeable

## Notes

- Tested 23 Sep 2026 alongside chipotle-adobo pork.
- The carrots were initially given too little time at 325°F and needed longer. For a shared 325°F oven, use roughly 50-60 minutes rather than the shorter roasting time used during the first cook.
- The 425°F method is preferred when oven scheduling allows because it produces better browning in less time.

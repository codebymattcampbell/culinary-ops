# Lemon-Garlic Mustard Greens

Blanched mustard greens finished in garlic butter and olive oil, with lemon for brightness.

## Status

**Experimental. Planned, not confirmed cooked.**

## Yield

- 2 large bunches; servings unconfirmed.

## Required Equipment

- Large pot, skillet, colander and bowl for an ice bath
- Stove / heat source

## Ingredients

- 2 large bunches mustard greens
- 2 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 tablespoon butter
- Approximately ¼ teaspoon kosher salt, adjusted to taste, plus salt for blanching water
- Black pepper
- Tiny pinch sugar
- Juice of ½ lemon, or approximately 1 teaspoon apple cider vinegar
- Ice and water

## Method

1. Strip thick center ribs, tear leaves into **2–3 inch pieces**, and wash thoroughly to remove grit.
2. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Prepare an ice bath.
3. Blanch greens for approximately **2 minutes**.
4. Transfer immediately to the ice bath for **1–2 minutes**.
5. Drain and squeeze out excess water.
6. Heat olive oil and butter in a skillet. Add garlic and cook approximately **30 seconds**.
7. Add greens and sauté approximately **4–6 minutes**.
8. Season with salt, pepper and a tiny pinch of sugar.
9. Turn off heat and finish with lemon juice or cider vinegar.

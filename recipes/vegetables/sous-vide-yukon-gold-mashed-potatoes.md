# Sous Vide Yukon Gold Mashed Potatoes

A reusable mashed potato side, cooked in a bag with butter and salt and finished with warm dairy.

## Status

**Experimental — UNTESTED.** Temperature and time are starting points for a trial.

## Yield

- Scale to the meal; potato, butter and dairy quantities and servings not established.

## Required Equipment

- Sous vide circulator and water vessel
- Bags rated for 194°F cooking, with a suitable sealing method
- Potato masher or ricer, bowl and pan for warming milk or cream

## Ingredients

- Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into approximately **1½-inch chunks**
- Butter and salt for the bag, to taste
- Warm milk or cream, added gradually to desired consistency

## Method — Proposed

1. Bag evenly cut potatoes with butter and salt in a relatively even layer. Keep submerged with water circulating around the bag.
2. Cook at **194°F**, starting with an estimated **75–90 minutes once the bath has returned to temperature**. Check that the centers crush easily; extend if still firm.
3. Transfer to a bowl and mash or rice while hot. Add warm milk or cream gradually; adjust salt.
4. Serve with the chosen main or gravy.

## Quality Targets

- Potatoes fully mashable through the center; a smooth, fluffy mash without firm pieces.
- Stop mashing once combined to avoid a gluey texture.

## Notes

- This schedule is untested; chunk size, bag thickness, load and bath recovery affect timing.
- For a shared bath, start [large-cut carrots](sous-vide-large-cut-carrots.md) at 185°F first, then raise to 194°F and add the potato bag. Count the potato trial interval after the bath recovers.
- Carrots and potatoes have separate doneness checks. Remove either when ready; extend the other as needed.
- Butter and dairy proportions should be recorded during the first test rather than presented as finalized quantities.

## Variables to Record

- Potato weight, chunk dimensions, butter and salt amounts
- Bath recovery, actual time to mashable texture, dairy amount and final consistency

# Sous Vide Large-Cut Carrots

A reusable carrot side with an optional gentle gravy finish.

## Status

**Experimental — UNTESTED.** This is a proposed two-temperature schedule, not a confirmed result.

## Yield

- Scale to the meal; batch size and servings not established.

## Required Equipment

- Sous vide circulator and water vessel
- Bags rated for the cooking temperatures, with a suitable sealing method
- Small pan for optional gravy finish

## Ingredients

- Carrots, peeled and cut into large, reasonably uniform pieces
- Butter and salt, to taste; quantities to establish during testing
- Optional gravy from the chosen main

## Method — Proposed

1. Bag the carrots with butter and salt in an even layer; keep the bag submerged and allow water to circulate.
2. Start at **185°F for roughly 45–60 minutes**.
3. Raise the same bath to **194°F**. Once it reaches temperature, add the separately bagged [Yukon Gold potatoes](sous-vide-yukon-gold-mashed-potatoes.md), if making them.
4. Trial a further **60–90 minutes at 194°F** for the carrots. Begin checking near the early end and remove them when tender; the extra time is an estimate, not a required minimum regardless of texture.
5. If desired, finish or reheat gently in gravy shortly before serving. Avoid prolonged simmering once tender.

## Quality Targets

- Tender throughout while retaining their shape, without a hard center or mushy edges.

## Notes

- Large-cut thickness is not yet specified, so the schedule cannot be treated as precise.
- The bath's heating interval adds cooking time for carrots but is not included in the proposed 194°F interval.
- Potatoes may need longer than the carrots. Remove each bag when its own texture is right; do not force both to finish together.
- If using with a braise, schedule around observed roast tenderness rather than a fixed serving-time promise.

## Variables to Record

- Cut dimensions, batch size, bath recovery time and texture at each check
- Actual time at each temperature and result after gravy reheating

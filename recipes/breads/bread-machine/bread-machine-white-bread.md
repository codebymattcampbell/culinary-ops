# Bread Machine White Bread

A simple white sandwich loaf for the **Zojirushi BB-PDC20**, starting with a water-based baseline formula so later changes can be compared against a known first test.

| Recipe Details | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | **Experimental** |
| Yield | 1 bread-machine loaf |
| Machine | Zojirushi BB-PDC20 |
| Course | **1 — Basic White** |
| Crust | **Medium** |

## Required Equipment

- [Bread Machine](../../../equipment/bread-machine.md) — Zojirushi BB-PDC20

## Ingredients

- 1 cup + 2 tbsp lukewarm water
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 3 cups bread flour
- 2 tsp bread-machine or instant yeast

> **Baseline note:** Keep the volume measurements above for this first test because that is how the loaf was actually loaded on 26 Sep 2026. Convert to weighed measurements only after the result is recorded so the original test remains reproducible.

## Method

### 1. Install the Paddles

Install both kneading paddles before adding ingredients.

### 2. Load the Bread Machine

Add ingredients in this order:

1. Water
2. Butter
3. Sugar
4. Salt
5. Bread flour
6. Yeast in a shallow well on top of the flour

Keep the yeast dry and separated from the salt and liquid until mixing begins.

### 3. Select the Program

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Course | **1 — Basic White** |
| Crust | **Medium** |
| Delay timer | **Off** |

Press **Start**.

### 4. Check the Dough

Inspect the dough about **5–10 minutes into active kneading**.

| Observation | Action |
|---|---|
| Smooth, soft ball that mostly clears the pan | Make no adjustment |
| Wet or smearing heavily | Add bread flour **1 tbsp at a time** |
| Dry, crumbly, or stiff | Add water **1 tbsp at a time** |

The target is a smooth, elastic dough that is slightly tacky but cohesive.

### 5. Finish and Cool

1. Remove the loaf promptly when the cycle completes.
2. Turn it onto a wire rack.
3. Cool before slicing so the crumb can set.

## Quality Targets

- **Rise:** Even, supported loaf without collapse
- **Crust:** Even medium browning without becoming hard
- **Crumb:** Soft, even, and suitable for sandwiches
- **Texture:** Moist but not gummy
- **Flavor:** Clean, lightly sweet white-bread flavor
- **Bottom:** No dense or compressed band

## Variables to Record

- Dough consistency during the first knead
- Any flour or water correction
- Final loaf height and top shape
- Crust thickness and color
- Paddle-hole size and location
- Crumb softness and evenness
- Flavor
- Cooling time before slicing
- Day-2 texture, if any remains

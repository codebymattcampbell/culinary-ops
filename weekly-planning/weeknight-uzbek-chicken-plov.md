# Weeknight Uzbek Chicken Plov

- **Recipe status:** New weekly draft
- **Cook day:** Unassigned
- **Yield:** 5 servings
- **WW guidance:** Moderate
- **Estimated calories:** 525-575 per serving
- **Hands-on / total time:** 25 minutes / 55 minutes
- **Equipment:** Dutch oven or heavy pot with a tight lid

This weeknight version preserves the characteristic plov structure: chicken and onions form the base, carrots create the middle layer, and rice steams on top without being stirred until the end.

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups long-grain rice, rinsed until the water is mostly clear
- 1½ lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1½-inch pieces
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 large yellow onion, sliced
- 1 lb carrots, cut into thin matchsticks
- 1 whole head garlic, loose outer paper removed and top trimmed
- 1½ tsp cumin seeds or 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- 1½ tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 2¼ cups low-sodium chicken broth, warmed
- ⅓ cup raisins, optional

## Method

1. Rinse the rice and let it drain. Heat the oil in the Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
2. Brown the chicken for 5-6 minutes. Add the onion and cook for another 4 minutes.
3. Add cumin, coriander, paprika, salt, and pepper. Spread the carrots over the chicken in an even layer. Do not stir after this point.
4. Spread the rice over the carrots. Press the garlic head into the center and scatter raisins over the rice, if using.
5. Pour warm broth gently around the edges. Bring to a simmer, cover tightly, and reduce the heat to low.
6. Cook for 20 minutes. Turn off the heat and rest, still covered, for 10 minutes.
7. Remove the garlic. Fluff the rice, then fold all layers together. Squeeze softened garlic cloves into individual servings as desired.

# Egg Lemon-Oregano Chicken Thighs with Bagged Greek Salad

- **Recipe status:** New weekly draft
- **Cook day:** Unassigned
- **Yield:** 4-5 servings
- **WW guidance:** Yes
- **Estimated calories:** 450-525 per serving, depending on the salad kit
- **Hands-on / total time:** 15 minutes / about 55 minutes
- **Equipment:** Big Green Egg, heat deflector, instant-read thermometer, mixing bowl

This is the low-effort Egg cook for the week. The chicken seasons while the Egg heats, so no advance marinating is required.

## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, trimmed
- 1½ tbsp olive oil
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 4 garlic cloves, finely grated, or 4 tsp jarred minced garlic
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp smoked paprika

### Salad

- 2 Greek or Mediterranean chopped salad kits, 10-12 oz each
- Remaining lemon wedges, for serving

## Method

1. Set up the Egg for indirect cooking with the heat deflector. Stabilize at 400°F.
2. Mix the olive oil, lemon zest, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt, pepper, and paprika. Coat the chicken and let it stand while the Egg heats.
3. Place the thighs smooth-side up on the grate. Cook for 15 minutes.
4. Flip the thighs and cook for another 10-15 minutes. Begin checking early and remove each piece when its thickest part reaches 175°F.
5. Rest the chicken for 5 minutes. Toss the salad kits while it rests.
6. Divide the chicken and salad among 4-5 plates. Serve with lemon wedges.

## Execution Timeline

| Time before serving | Action |
|---:|---|
| 55 min | Light Egg and establish indirect 400°F setup |

## Leftovers

Refrigerate chicken separately from dressed salad. Use within 3-4 days. Reheat the chicken gently in a covered skillet, microwave, or 300°F oven.

# Spanish Chickpea, Spinach, Potato and Egg Stew

- **Recipe status:** New weekly draft
- **Cook day:** Unassigned
- **Yield:** 4 servings
- **WW guidance:** Yes
- **Estimated calories:** 410-460 per serving
- **Hands-on / total time:** 20 minutes / 50 minutes
- **Equipment:** Dutch oven or deep skillet with lid; small saucepan

This is a weeknight version of Spanish *potaje de garbanzos y espinacas*. Chickpeas, spinach, tomato, smoked paprika, and cumin are characteristic; eggs make it a complete dinner.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 can diced or crushed tomatoes, 14-15 oz
- 1 lb Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into ½-inch cubes
- 2 cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth or water
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- ¼ tsp red-pepper flakes
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- 10-12 oz fresh spinach
- 1 tbsp red-wine vinegar
- 4 large eggs

## Method

1. Place the eggs in a small saucepan, cover with cold water, and bring to a boil. Cover, turn off the heat, and rest for 10 minutes. Chill in cold water and peel.
2. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat. Cook the onion with a pinch of salt for 5 minutes.
3. Add the garlic, tomato paste, paprika, cumin, and pepper flakes. Stir for 60 seconds.
4. Add the tomatoes, potatoes, chickpeas, broth, bay leaf, and salt. Cover and simmer for 18-22 minutes, until the potatoes are tender.
5. Add spinach by handfuls and cook for 3-5 minutes. Stir in the vinegar. Taste for salt and acidity.
6. Quarter the eggs and place one egg over each serving.

# Polish Kielbasa, Cabbage and White Bean Sheet-Pan Supper

- **Recipe status:** New weekly draft
- **Cook day:** Unassigned
- **Yield:** 4-5 servings
- **WW guidance:** Moderate
- **Estimated calories:** 375-450 per serving
- **Hands-on / total time:** 10 minutes / 35 minutes
- **Equipment:** Oven, large rimmed sheet pan, mixing bowl

Turkey kielbasa and canned white beans keep this meal protein-forward without requiring separate pans. Bagged cabbage eliminates nearly all vegetable preparation.

## Ingredients

### Sheet Pan

- 1 package turkey kielbasa, about 14 oz, sliced ½ inch thick
- 2 cans cannellini or other white beans, 15 oz each, drained and rinsed
- 1 bag coleslaw mix or shredded cabbage, about 14 oz
- 1 medium red onion, thinly sliced
- 1½ tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp caraway seeds
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp kosher salt

### Mustard Finish

- 1½ tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1½ tbsp apple-cider vinegar
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tbsp water

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. On the sheet pan, toss the cabbage and onion with olive oil, caraway, paprika, pepper, and salt. Spread into an even layer.
3. Roast for 10 minutes.
4. Stir the cabbage, then add the kielbasa and beans. Roast for another 12-15 minutes, until the kielbasa is browned at the edges and the cabbage is tender with crisp spots.
5. Mix the Dijon, vinegar, honey, and water.
6. Drizzle the mustard mixture over the hot sheet pan, toss, and serve.

## Execution Timeline

| Time before serving | Action |
|---:|---|
| 35 min | Heat oven to 425°F |
| 30 min | Assemble seasoned cabbage and onion on sheet pan |
| 25 min | Begin roasting |
| 15 min | Add kielbasa and beans; mix mustard finish |
| 2 min | Toss with mustard mixture |
| Serve | Divide among 4-5 plates |

## Leftovers

Refrigerate for up to 3-4 days. Reheat uncovered in a 400°F oven or in a skillet so the kielbasa and cabbage regain some browning.

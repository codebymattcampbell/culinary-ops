# Filipino Ground Pork Adobo Bowls with Bagged Slaw and Rice

- **Recipe status:** New weekly draft
- **Cook day:** Unassigned
- **Yield:** 5 servings
- **WW guidance:** Yes-ish
- **Estimated calories:** 450-500 per serving
- **Hands-on / total time:** 15 minutes / 30 minutes
- **Equipment:** Rice cooker or small Instant Pot, large skillet

This is a weeknight adaptation of Filipino adobo flavors, not a traditional whole-cut adobo. Ground pork keeps the cook fast, while vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, bay leaf, and black pepper provide the characteristic savory-tart profile.

## Ingredients

### Rice

- 1½ cups dry long-grain rice
- Water according to rice-cooker directions

### Pork Adobo

- 1¼ lb lean ground pork
- 1½ cups frozen diced onion, or 1 medium yellow onion diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced, or 4 tsp jarred minced garlic
- ⅓ cup low-sodium soy sauce
- ¼ cup cane vinegar, rice vinegar, or apple-cider vinegar
- ½ cup water
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp neutral oil, only if the skillet is dry

### Quick Slaw

- 1 bag coleslaw mix, about 14 oz
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar or apple-cider vinegar
- 1 tsp sugar
- ¼ tsp kosher salt

## Method

1. Start the rice.
2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high. Add the pork and onion. Cook for 6-8 minutes, breaking the pork into small pieces, until no pink remains. Drain excess rendered fat if needed.
3. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
4. Add soy sauce, vinegar, water, bay leaves, brown sugar, and pepper. Simmer uncovered for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the liquid lightly coats the pork.
5. While the pork simmers, toss the coleslaw mix with vinegar, sugar, and salt.
6. Remove the bay leaves. Divide rice among five bowls and top with pork adobo and slaw.

## Execution Timeline

| Time before serving | Action |
|---:|---|
| 30 min | Start rice |
| 25 min | Brown pork and onion |
| 17 min | Add garlic and adobo liquid |
| 12 min | Mix quick slaw |
| 2 min | Remove bay leaves and assemble bowls |

## Leftovers

Store rice, pork, and slaw separately. Refrigerate for up to 3-4 days. Reheat the rice and pork until steaming hot; keep the slaw cold.

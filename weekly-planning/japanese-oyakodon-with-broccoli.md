# Japanese Oyakodon with Broccoli

- **Recipe status:** New weekly draft
- **Cook day:** Unassigned
- **Yield:** 5 servings
- **WW guidance:** Yes
- **Estimated calories:** 500-550 per serving
- **Hands-on / total time:** 20 minutes / 45 minutes
- **Equipment:** Rice cooker; 12-inch nonstick skillet with lid

Oyakodon means "parent-and-child bowl," referring to chicken and egg. The chicken and onion simmer in a lightly sweet dashi-soy broth before barely beaten egg is added in two stages.

## Ingredients

### Rice and Broccoli

- 1½ cups dry long-grain or Japanese short-grain rice
- Water according to rice-cooker directions
- 1 lb frozen broccoli
- ½ tsp kosher salt

### Chicken and Egg

- 1½ lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 2 medium yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 1½ cups prepared dashi
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- ¼ cup mirin
- 2 tsp sugar
- 6 large eggs, lightly beaten but still visibly streaky
- 4 scallions, thinly sliced

## Method

1. Start the rice. Steam or microwave the broccoli shortly before serving and season lightly.
2. Combine dashi, soy sauce, mirin, and sugar in the skillet. Add the onions and simmer for 5 minutes.
3. Add the chicken, cover, and simmer for 8-10 minutes, stirring once, until the pieces reach at least 165°F.
4. Pour about two-thirds of the eggs evenly over the chicken. Cover and cook over medium-low heat for 60-90 seconds.
5. Add the remaining egg and scallions. Cover for another 30-60 seconds. The egg should be softly set, not dry.
6. Divide rice among five bowls and spoon the chicken, egg, onions, and broth over it. Serve broccoli alongside.

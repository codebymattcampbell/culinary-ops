# Lemon-Rosemary Shrimp, Cannellini Beans and Zucchini

- **Recipe status:** New weekly draft
- **Cook day:** Unassigned
- **Yield:** 5 servings
- **WW guidance:** Yes
- **Estimated calories:** 300-350 per serving
- **Hands-on / total time:** 20 minutes / 35 minutes
- **Equipment:** Oven; large rimmed sheet pan

This is adapted and scaled from WW's lemon-rosemary shrimp, zucchini, and white-bean sheet-pan recipe. The empty sheet pan preheats with the oven so the zucchini browns instead of steaming.

## Ingredients

- Cooking spray
- 4 medium zucchini, sliced ½ inch thick
- 1 medium yellow onion, roughly chopped
- ¾ tsp kosher salt, divided
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1¼ lb large raw shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 1½ tbsp finely chopped fresh rosemary
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- Zest of 2 lemons
- ¼ cup lemon juice

## Method

1. Place the empty sheet pan in the oven and heat to 450°F.
2. Toss the zucchini and onion with ½ teaspoon salt and the black pepper.
3. Carefully remove the hot pan, coat it with cooking spray, and spread the vegetables in a single layer. Roast for 8 minutes.
4. Toss the shrimp and beans with rosemary, garlic, and the remaining salt.
5. Stir the vegetables toward the edges and place the shrimp mixture in the center. Roast for 6-8 minutes, until the shrimp are opaque and curled.
6. Add the lemon zest and juice immediately after removing the pan. Toss and serve.

# Turkish-Style Beef Kofta Pitas

## Ingredients

### Kofta

- 1¼ lb 93% lean ground beef
- ½ medium yellow onion, grated and squeezed dry
- 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
- ½ cup finely chopped parsley
- 1½ tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- ½ tsp ground allspice
- ½ tsp Aleppo pepper or smoked paprika
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp olive oil, for the cooking surface

### Garlic Yogurt

- 1 cup nonfat plain Greek yogurt
- 1 garlic clove, finely grated
- Juice of ½ lemon
- 2 tbsp chopped mint or dill
- ¼ tsp kosher salt

### Salad and Serving

- 2 English cucumbers, diced
- 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
- ½ large red onion, thinly sliced
- Juice of ½ lemon
- 1 tbsp chopped parsley
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- 5 whole-wheat pitas

## Method

1. Gently mix all kofta ingredients. Divide into 10 small flattened logs or patties. Refrigerate for 10 minutes while preparing the sauce and salad.
2. Mix the yogurt sauce. In another bowl, combine the cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, lemon, parsley, and salt.
3. Heat the skillet or Blackstone to medium-high, about 400°F surface temperature. Lightly oil it.
4. Cook the kofta for 3-4 minutes per side, until browned and at least 160°F internally.
5. Warm the pitas briefly. Serve two kofta per pita with salad and garlic yogurt.
